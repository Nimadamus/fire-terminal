Where FIRE is installed

Replacing a computer? Remove the old one here and the seat is free immediately. Removing a computer does not cancel anything and does not touch your exchange account.

Cancelling does not close your positions. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. After that FIRE stops sending orders and tells you why. Anything you hold stays open at your exchange, where you manage it as normal.

Lost your key? Email us from the address you bought with and we will resend it. Never send a licence key or an API key to anybody who asks you for it.
