## r/SideProject

**Title:** I built a Windows terminal for trading short duration event contracts

Body:

> I trade 15 minute crypto event contracts, and most of what I lost was going
> to my own execution: the mistyped size, the order that filled worse than the

> So I built FIRE. Every open market on one screen, index price and the level

## BetaList

Product: **FIRE**
Tagline: Every market on one screen. One click to take a side.
URL: https://fire-terminal.onrender.com
Category: Finance, Developer tools

Description:

> FIRE is a Windows desktop terminal for trading short duration event contracts.
> It shows every open market at once with live prices and order book depth,
> prices orders off the real book, and refuses any order whose worst case loss
> exceeds a ceiling you set. It gives no signals and no recommendations: it
> places the order you click. A free demo mode with simulated markets needs no
> exchange account. Currently pre-launch, pending exchange authorisation.
