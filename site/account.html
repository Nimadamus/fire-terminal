Your subscription

Manage your subscription

Change your card, download invoices, or cancel. Enter the licence key you use in FIRE and we will take you to the billing portal.

Cancelling does not close your positions. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. After that FIRE stops sending orders and tells you why. Anything you hold stays open at your exchange, where you manage it as normal.

Lost your key? Email us from the address you bought with and we will resend it. Never send a licence key or an API key to anyone who asks for it.
